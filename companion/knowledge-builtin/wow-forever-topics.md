### The Hall of Thanes (es: El Salón de los Feudales)
keywords: Hall of Thanes, HoT, Salón de los Feudales, Old Ironforge, Antigua Forjaz, Ironforge, Forjaz, Dun Morogh, Durgen Dirgehammer, Durgen Martillo Funesto, Faldrim Anvilmar, Magmatus, Plunder
- Level 13-18 (Blizz). Beneath Ironforge, Dun Morogh. Alliance counterpart to Ragefire Chasm. WH zone 16919 "contested dungeon".
- Entrance: in Ironforge, walk into The High Seat (Magni's throne room); passage immediately on your LEFT (spiderweb-covered, ~43.5,52.0 Ironforge map per WarcraftTavern) winds down through Old Ironforge to the portal. Lava below: falling in is lethal.
- Horde: very long trek (no quick travel): Tarren Mill -> Arathi Highlands -> Wetlands -> Loch Modan -> Dun Morogh -> through Ironforge (guards will kill you; strip gear to save durability). Alt: boat Southshore -> Menethil Harbor.
- Bosses in order (WH+fextralife+WT agree): 1) Faldrim Anvilmar (Anvilmar's Rest; patrols; Mind Blast, 10-min Curse of Anvilmar on tank: -20% attack/cast speed); 2) Magmatus (with Dark Iron Summoner add - kill add first; fire pulse, Combust on random player); 3) Plunder (stone golem, patrols hallway; knockback - tank against wall); 4) Durgen Dirgehammer (final; Reliquary of Kings; AoE Fear + Rend; 2 Lesser Stone Golems - kill first). (es: Durgen Martillo Funesto)
- Undead mobs and golems are immune to bleeds (fextralife).
- Loot (fextralife; WH confirms Fang of Magmatus = blue dagger ilvl 18, req 13, drops from Magmatus): Faldrim: Aetherwisp Bracers, Ephemeral Choker, Spiritwraith Drape. Magmatus: Kindlegem Girdle, Fang of Magmatus (dagger), Flamefist Grips. Plunder: Golemguard Chest, Golemheart Stave, Treads of the Protector Golem. Durgen: Direhammer Leggings, Durgen's Crescent Axe, Robes of the Disgraced Thane.
- Quests (mostly Alliance-only; 1 shared): An Ancient Grudge (Ghostly Attendant inside, Anvilmar's Rest - both factions); Important Heirlooms (Thom Filch, Ironforge ~33,45, just outside dungeon); The Restless Dead (Afadra Dunwall, Ironforge ~34,49); Old Ironforge Incursion (Earthseer Farsen, Dun Morogh, Gol'Golar Quarry ~65,58; needs Dark Iron Map from Dark Iron Spy ~77,60 Dun Morogh); The Treaty of Understanding (tablet in vault in final boss room; turn in to King Magni). Quest levels differ between guides (WH 9-10, fextralife 14-16).

- Loot (fextralife; WH confirms Ponderous Orb = blue off-hand ilvl 35): Atrexis: Bonebinder's Signet, Gravespike Repeater (crossbow), Graveweave Bindings. ### XP, rested and group rules
keywords: experiencia, XP, rested, descansado, saco de dormir, Cozy Sleeping Bag, group XP, XP en grupo, boosting, quest XP
- XP to level (WT): 1 to 2 needs 400. 10 to 11 needs 7,600. 30 to 31 needs 47,400. 59 to 60 needs 209,800. Total to 30 is 499,000 and total to 60 is 4,084,700.
- Rested XP: you gain 5% of a level per 8 hours in an inn or capital city, capped at 150% of a level (30 bubbles). It doubles kill XP only.
- Forever-only rested sources: the Cozy Sleeping Bag counts as an inn. It also gives up to 3 Well-Rested stacks (+1% kill XP each) that persist through death. The Camp Tent gives a 1-hour rested-like buff. The Legacy "Well Rested" perk adds 4% per rank to rest gain and to the cap.
- Well Fed from cooking gives +5% kill XP for about 15 minutes.
- Group kill XP: each of 5 members gets the solo XP divided by 5, times 1.4. Quest turn-in XP is not reduced by grouping. Raid groups do not get quest credit.
- Since the Oct 8 beta build, the level penalty uses each recipient's own level (Classic Era method).
- Dungeon kill XP: since Nov 14 (Blizz "Player-Level Dungeon XP Adjustment"), no one in the party gets XP from an enemy when a party member is more than 12 levels above it. This blocks boosting. The Nov 13 rule (no XP if the enemy was gray to anyone) "missed the mark" and was replaced.
- Dungeon quests give more XP than in Classic. They were halved from the beta value on Oct 1 and still pay more than double Classic (per guides, Method).
- Quests 5+ levels below you lose 10-20% XP per extra level. Exploration gives a little XP.
- At 60 there is no XP-to-gold conversion. Since Nov 10, most quests of level 48+ also reward gold.
- There are no level boosts and no catch-up mechanics. Developers have said they avoid catch-up (kami-labs).

### Legacy system (overview and points)
keywords: Legacy, Legado, legacy points, puntos de legado, account-wide progression, progresión de cuenta, Legacy Challenges, desafíos de legado, hotkey Y
- Account-wide horizontal progression with 3 parts: Legacy Challenges, Legacy Perks and Legacy Rewards. Open it with the shield icon or the Y key (Blizzard).
- It unlocks with your first Legacy Point. Usually that's when your first character reaches level 25, but it can also come from 150 in a non-gathering primary profession or from exploring the whole world map (Blizzard).
- 65 challenges, each worth 1 point. Points are shared across the account, but each character spends them separately (Blizzard).
  - Classes, 27 points: reach level 25, 45 and 60 on each of the 9 classes.
  - Tradeskills, 18 points: 150, 225 and 300 in Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring.
  - PvP, 12 points: Honor ranks 3, 7, 10, 13 and 14; Exalted with the Alterac Valley, Arathi Basin, Warsong Gulch and Darkspear Islands factions; Field of Honor Journey weeks 4, 7 and 10 (Warcraft Tavern breakdown).
  - Adventure, 2 points: explore the whole world map on one character; finish the Lord Valthalak questline ("An Earnest Proposition" through "Saving the Best for Last").
  - Dungeons, 3 points: Novice, Experienced and Master Spelunker, for the final bosses of the 15-25, 26-45 and 46-60 dungeons. Nov 13 hotfix: Balnazzar (Stratholme) now counts for Master Spelunker.
  - Raids, 3 points: Onyxia; all 13 Hyjal Summit bosses; all 8 Barrow Deeps bosses. Raids open Dec 9, 2026.
- SPENDING CAP: 16 points per character at launch. Blizzard says one character alone can reach up to 29 points without alts, and the cap will rise in later updates.
- Hardcore: points earned on Hardcore count on all other rulesets, but not the other way round. PvP challenges can't be done on Hardcore, which will have 12 challenges of its own (Blizzard).
- Rewards are account-wide cosmetics claimed from Innkeeper Wiley in Ratchet (NPC per Warcraft Tavern): 15 points Replica Ironforge Air Rifle (toy), 25 Spectral Bear Cub (pet), 40 Spectral Bear Tabard, 55 Reins of the Spectral Bear (100% speed mount). More rewards will come each season.

### Legacy perks (3 trees; ranks per Warcraft Tavern, names per Blizzard)
keywords: legacy perks, ventajas de legado, legacy talents, talentos de legado, Bartering, Well Rested, Talented, Frequent Flier, Reagent Economy
- Some perks need 5 or 10 points spent in their tree first (Method). Builds are per character.
- Professions tree:
  - Working Overtime (5 ranks): +4% chance per rank to gain skill from crafting.
  - Bountiful Harvest (5 ranks): +20% per rank Scarce materials from Mining, Herbalism and Skinning.
  - Luremaster (2 ranks): 25% chance per rank of an extra fish when fishing with a lure.
  - Performance Bonus (3 ranks): 5% chance per rank of doubled Merchant's Favor on crate turn-ins.
  - Bartering (2 ranks): -5% per rank on vendor prices. Does not apply to training or repairs.
  - Master Chef (5 ranks): 10% chance per rank of an extra cooking result.
  - Dedicated Study (1 rank, daily): +1 to your lowest primary or secondary skill. Once all are at 300, it gives 2-4 random Elemental Essences instead.
- Adventure tree:
  - Well Rested (5 ranks): +4% per rank to rested XP accumulation and to the rested cap.
  - Thrill of Adventure (5 ranks): after a non-trivial kill, restore 1% per rank of health and mana over 10 s. Not in dungeons, raids or battlegrounds.
  - Field Medicine (2 ranks): Recently Bandaged lasts 5 s less per rank (open world only).
  - Field Guide (3 ranks): -8% per rank on the camp feature cooldown.
  - Talented (5 ranks): talent points start up to 5 levels earlier.
  - High Alert (2 ranks): better stealth detection (not in battlegrounds).
  - Frequent Flier (1 rank): -50% flight path cost and 20% faster flights.
- Resourcefulness tree:
  - Gourmand (3 ranks): +33% per rank food buff duration.
  - The Quick and the Dead (2 ranks): +5% speed while dead; helpful spells are free for 1 min after resurrection.
  - Reinforce (5 ranks): -8% per rank durability loss on death.
  - Diplomat (5 ranks): +2% per rank reputation gain.
  - Permanence (2 ranks): long class buffs and camp benefits last +50%.
  - For Great Honor (5 ranks): +2% per rank Honor.
  - Reagent Economy (1 rank): class spells need no vendor reagents, and tier 1 camp features need no materials.
- Popular builds (Method): leveling = Thrill of Adventure 5 + Well Rested + Talented; gathering = Bountiful Harvest + Bartering + Dedicated Study + Master Chef + Luremaster; dungeons and raids = Reinforce + Diplomat + Quick and the Dead + Reagent Economy + Gourmand.

### Camping and campfires
keywords: camping, acampada, campfire, hoguera, campsite, campamento, camp features, funciones de acampada, Basic Campfire, Hoguera básica, Journeyman Campfire, Hoguera de oficial, Expert Campfire, Hoguera de experto, The Great Outdoors
- The Cooking campfire becomes a shared campsite. Players sit or craft nearby for 1 minute to get the benefits of every camp feature placed there (WH tooltip). Buffs work while sitting, in chairs, sleeping, lying down or kneeling (beta notes).
- Intro quest "The Great Outdoors" at about level 5 (Method). Profession "Camping 101: <profession>" quests teach the tier 1 objects (e.g. Camping 101: First Aid, Camping 101: Herbalism).
- Basic Campfire (es: Hoguera básica), WH spell 818: Cooking 1, Simple Wood plus Flint and Tinder, 10 s cast, 5 min cooldown. +4 Spirit and allows cooking. Holds 3 features. Burns about 10 minutes (Warcraft Tavern).
- Journeyman Campfire Kit (es: Equipo de hoguera de oficial), WH 279961: Cooking 90 (lowered from 140 in the Nov 10 hotfix), uses Star Wood (es: Madera de estrella), holds up to 5 features, 5 min cooldown. The blueprint (es: Plano: hoguera de oficial, WH 273087) needs Cooking 90 and drops from Lord Serpentis, Wailing Caverns (about 6%).
- Expert Campfire Kit (es: Equipo de hoguera de experto), WH 279974: Cooking 220 to use, uses Thick Logs (es: Troncos gruesos), holds up to 10 features. The blueprint (WH 273101) needs Cooking 200 to learn and drops from Slavecrusher Grox (about 4%).
- Each player can add ONE feature per campfire. All camping features share a 1-hour cooldown (WH Camp Tent tooltip). A higher tier of the same object can replace a lower one without using a slot.
- Feature tiers by profession skill: T1 = 20, T2 = 140, T3 = 300 (Leatherworking Tanning Rack lowered to 125 on Nov 6).
- Buffs are "mutually exclusive" with the class buff they copy and slightly weaker (Fish Bowl +8% stats vs Kings 10%). Camp benefits last 1 hour (2 hours with Pouch of Violet Dust).
- Objects (T1 / T2 / T3) and effects:
  - Alchemy: Mana Well (mana regen, like Wisdom) / Fermenter / Alchemy Laboratory (flasks, no Scholomance trip needed).
  - Blacksmithing: Sharpening Wheel (Strength, like Strength of Earth) / Anvil / Master Forge.
  - Enchanting: Enchanted Lute (stats, armor, resists, like Mark of the Wild) / Arcane Salvager (disenchant bonus; needs Runed Golden Rod) / Arcane Forge.
  - Engineering: Reagent Bot (reagent vendor, sells junk) / Repair Bot (repairs) / Anarchist's Workbench. No buff.
  - First Aid: First Aid Kit (Stamina, like Fortitude) / Toxin Study (healing potions and anti-venom) / Plague Doctor's Laboratory.
  - Fishing: Fish Bowl (+8% stats, like Kings) / Fishing Rack (lures, uncommon fish) / Fishing Hut (rare fish).
  - Herbalism: Incense Candle (Intellect, like Arcane Intellect, +2 to +25 by level) / Greenhouse (plant seeds) / Seed Hybridizer.
  - Leatherworking: Camp Tent (es: Tienda de campamento) / Tanning Rack / Sewing Machine.
  - Mining: Lodestone (attack power, like Might) / Rock Garden (spawns ore node) / Molten Foundry.
  - Skinning: Camp Chair (+2% crit, like Leader of the Pack/Moonkin) / Field Guide (Track Beasts) / Trapper's Workbench.
  - Tailoring: Faction Banner (Spirit, like Divine Spirit) / Spinning Wheel / Loom.
  - Cooking: campfires, Cookie's Feast (Stamina food), Iron Oven.
- Camp Tent (WH 279978, Leatherworking 20, 5 Light Leather): raises your rested XP to 5% of a level if you have less than that; once per hour ("Boosted Rest" debuff); needs a campfire nearby.
- Pouch of Violet Dust: toy that doubles camp benefit duration (2 h instead of 1) for everyone at the camp. It does not affect the Camp Tent. It was a Twitch drop (ended Nov 13) and will be removed in early December (Method).
- Legacy perks: Field Guide shortens the feature cooldown, Permanence makes camp buffs last +50%, and Reagent Economy makes T1 features free.
- Campfires don't damage players below level 5 (beta notes).

### Rested XP, Well Fed XP and the Cozy Sleeping Bag
keywords: rested XP, experiencia por descanso, descansado, Well Fed, bien alimentado, food XP, comida experiencia, Cozy Sleeping Bag, Saco de dormir acogedor, Well-Rested, XP bonus
- Well Fed (WH food tooltips; es: "bien alimentado"): eat at least 10 s for a 15-minute stat buff plus +5% XP from KILLS. It does not boost quest XP and is lost on death (Warcraft Tavern). Most cooked food gives it (Blizzard deep dive, Sep 13). Example: Brilliant Smallfish (es: Pececito luminoso) gives +2 attack power, Spiced Wolf Meat gives +1 Agility, Fillet of Frenzy gives +5 Fishing. Many recipes were retuned around one primary stat.
- Rested XP: 5% of a level per 8 hours in an inn or capital, capped at 150% of a level. It doubles kill XP and does not affect quest XP (Warcraft Tavern). Legacy Well Rested gives +4% per rank to both rate and cap.
- Cozy Sleeping Bag (es: Saco de dormir acogedor), WH 211527: unique BoP, 1-hour cooldown. Each minute inside gives one Well-Rested stack (+1% XP), up to 3 (+3%). The bag counts as a rested area. It can't be used in cities or inns, any party member can use it, and it disappears if you go about 100 yards away. It stacks with Well Fed and camp buffs. You get it from a no-combat chain of clickable objects starting at level 14: Westfall Nailed Plank 37,50, then Barrens Burned-Out Remains 46,74 (Horde starts here), Stonetalon, Loch Modan Carved Figurine 49,12, and finally Hillsbrad Thoradin Wall ("This Must Be the Place") (Warcraft Tavern). Since Nov 17 it gives a Resting aura and can't be used while mounted (Blizz).
- Student Fodder gives no XP bonus in Forever (Warcraft Tavern).
- XP curve unchanged per Blizzard: 4,084,700 total XP to reach 60 (Warcraft Tavern).
- Dungeon quests give 50% less bonus XP (beta). Nov 9 hotfix: dungeon kill XP +50%. Since Nov 14: no kill XP for the party when a member is more than 12 levels above the enemy (anti-boosting).
- At level 60, quest XP no longer converts to gold. To compensate, most quests of level 48+ now give gold (Nov 10 hotfix).

### Herbalism, Mining and Skinning (gathering)
keywords: herbalism, herboristería, mining, minería, skinning, desollar, scarce materials, materiales escasos, seeds, semillas, Greenhouse, Rock Garden
- "Scarce" materials are a new rarity, boosted by the Legacy perk Bountiful Harvest. Ore nodes spawn faster when more players are in an area (Warcraft Tavern).
- Herbalism:
  - Seeds drop from herb nodes.
  - The Greenhouse (T2, skill 140; blueprint drops from Shadetooth, Excavation Site: Wetlands, about 8%) grows herbs from seeds. It costs 2 Simple Wood, Arthas' Tears and Purple Lotus, and lasts 15 minutes.
  - The Seed Hybridizer (T3, skill 300) multiplies seeds or upgrades them to a rarer tier. Its blueprint source is unknown (Method, Oct 28). Turned back on Nov 19 with lower values and a chance to fail; Scarce Herb Seeds can't go in it (Blizz).
- Mining: Rock Garden (T2) spawns a common ore node. Molten Foundry (T3) is needed for high-end smelting. Rockslicer (Deadmines, from Rhahk'Zor) mines low-level veins without the profession (30 uses).
- Skinning: a Skinning Knife in your bags is enough. Pristine Leather, Hide and Scale are Scarce drops used by many new recipes. A creature can be skinned only once.

### Crafting professions: notable Forever changes
keywords: alchemy changes, blacksmithing changes, engineering changes, enchanting changes, tailoring changes, leatherworking changes, Merchant's Favor recipes
- Alchemy:
  - No healing potions; they moved to First Aid.
  - Makes dyes, which some crafted items need.
  - Flasks are made at the camp Alchemy Laboratory instead of Scholomance.
  - New and changed recipes: Minor Arcane Elixir (spell power), Rage Potion usable by Druids, Elixir of the Grizzly (Strength + crit), Elixir of Cunning (Agility + Intellect), and Mender's, Spellblasting, Frenzy and Smog potions from Merchant's Favor.
  - "Elixir of Greater Firepower" was renamed "Elixir of Holy Power" (Nov 10 hotfix).
- Blacksmithing: hundreds of new plans (Merchant's Favor, dungeon drops). Sharpening stones and weightstones now stack with poisons, Windfury Weapon and Windfury Totem. Coarse Grinding Stone gives skill-ups up to 90.
- Enchanting:
  - Disenchanting needs uncommon (green) or better.
  - Necklace enchants are new (Strength, Spell Power, Healing Power, Agility, Deflection).
  - New weapon enchants: Insight, Recovery, Revelation.
  - Craftable staves, orbs and relics (idols, librams, totems) come from Merchant's Favor.
  - The Novice's Practice Wand is learnable at skill 1.
  - A level 1 character can learn Enchanting and disenchant any green (Method).
- Engineering:
  - "SAF-T" explosives can be used by non-engineers.
  - Satchel of Copper Bombs goes in the thrown slot and needs no ammo.
  - The Arclight Spanner is needed for complex recipes.
  - New items: Clanking Cord belts, Gnomish Poultryizer, Stealthman 52, Ultralight Goblin Glider (Merchant's Favor).
  - Camp objects give no buff (vendor and repair bots).
- Leatherworking: hundreds of new patterns. Dragonscale was reworked by role (Green = healer, Blue = caster, Black = physical). New Forceful and Mystic Rugged Armor Kits. Tanning Rack is at skill 125.
- Tailoring: reagent bags. Mooncloth is a bottleneck with a 4-day cooldown (per guides). Many new cloth patterns.

  - Craftsman's Writs drop from mobs and start a timed delivery quest (about 9 h) for reputation and silver.

### Best profession combos (Forever guides)
keywords: best professions, mejores profesiones, profesiones por clase, profession pairing, combos, gold professions
- General pairings (Warcraft Tavern): Herbalism + Alchemy (gold, self-sufficiency); Mining + Blacksmithing (plate); Mining + Engineering (PvP and utility); Tailoring + Enchanting (cloth casters); Skinning + Leatherworking (leather and mail).   - Hunter, Rogue: Leatherworking + Blacksmithing.
  - Shaman, Druid: Leatherworking + Tailoring.
  - Warrior, Paladin: Blacksmithing + Leatherworking.

- Per class, PvE/PvP view (MeinMMO, updated Nov 9):
  - Melee and hunters: Engineering + their armor profession.
  - Casters and priests: Tailoring + Enchanting.
  - PvP for everyone: Engineering + Alchemy or Mining.
- Gold while leveling: two gathering professions (Skinning + Mining or Herbalism) and drop them later (MeinMMO).

### Gold making by level (Forever guides)
