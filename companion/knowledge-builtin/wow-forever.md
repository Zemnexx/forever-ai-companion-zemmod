## World of Warcraft: Forever base guide

Facts from Blizzard and public guides, last reviewed on November 27, 2026 (23 days after launch; Blizzard hotfixes up to November 25). Anything marked "per guides" may change or be incomplete: if the answer depends on it and you can search the internet, verify it. Dates are absolute: compare them with today's date. Names in the Spanish client are given as "es: ...".

### What it is
- A permanent version of WoW released by Blizzard on November 4, 2026, alongside modern WoW and WoW Classic (it is not a mode or a Classic season). Included in the WoW subscription.
- Set in the first year of the original 2004 WoW, in its own continuity: after Warcraft III's "Forsaken Kingdom" campaign and before Molten Core.
- Level cap 60, with no increases planned. No flying mounts and no level scaling.
- Uses the modern client (interface 1.60, interface number 16001). The original 2004 content is still the base, but there are over 1,000 new quests and class and system changes: don't assume everything is the same as Classic.

### New zones
- Zephras Isle (es: Isla de Zephras): the Skyborne starting zone, levels 1-12. Skyboats from Valanaar go to Dalaran (Alterac Mountains) and to Mulgore north of Thunder Bluff.
- The Riverglades (esES: Clarofluvial): an Eastern Kingdoms east-coast zone, levels 36-44, over 150 quests, both factions. Alliance hub Farholde Keep, Horde hub Rog'mar. Reached by road from Redridge or the Badlands, or by boat from Steamwheedle Port (Tanaris) to Powderfuse Port. It holds Krol'dok Stronghold.
- Shen'dralas: a hidden area between Mulgore and Desolace, tied to the Shen'dralar, Eldre'Thalas (Dire Maul) and the centaurs.
- Mount Hyjal (es: Monte Hyjal): level 60 zone entered through Darkwhisper Gorge in Winterspring; entrance to the Hyjal Summit and The Barrow Deeps raids.
- Original zones with visual changes (rivers in Elwynn, fog in Darkshore, moonlight in Ashenvale).

### New dungeons (5 players; official Blizzard level ranges)
- Hall of Thanes (es: El Salón de los Feudales): under Ironforge, 13-18.
- Ruins of Lordaeron (es: Ruinas de Lordaeron): the ruins above Undercity, 15-20.
- Excavation Site: Wetlands (Whelgar's Excavation Site): Wetlands, 24-29.
- City of Dalaran (es: Ciudad de Dalaran): Alterac Mountains, 28-33; needs the Dalaran Sewer Key from a quest chain, entrance through the sewers on the Lordamere Lake shore.
- The Drowned City (esES: La Ciudad Sumergida; esMX: La Ciudad Anegada): off the northwest coast of Stranglethorn (es: Tuercespina), 35-40; underwater entrance.
- Krol'dok Stronghold (es: Bastión Krol'dok): The Riverglades, open-air dungeon, 40-45.
- Alcaz Prison (es: Prisión de Alcaz): Alcaz Island (Dustwallow Marsh, es: Marjal Revolcafango), 48-53.
- Blackmaw Hold (es: Bastión Faucenegra): Azshara, 55-60.
- Shaper's Terrace (es: El Bancal del Creador): Un'Goro Crater (es: Cráter de Un'Goro), 58-60.
- The original dungeons (Deadmines, Gnomeregan, Scarlet Monastery...) still exist. No automatic dungeon finder and no teleport: a Classic-style Looking For Group tool lists players and groups by dungeon. Meeting stones don't summon (only a warlock's Ritual of Summoning). Each dungeon can be reset and entered at most 5 times per hour (Blizzard, November 20; it was 10).

### Raids
- Open on December 9, 2026: The Barrow Deeps (10 players), Hyjal Summit (20 players) and Onyxia's Lair (40, needs the Drakefire Amulet in your bags from its attunement chain). Raids reset weekly.
- From November 4 to December 8, 2026 there are no WoW Forever raids available. From December 9 on there are: if today is that date or later, they are open (search their current state if it matters). As of November 27, no time of day, reset day or loot rules were announced. World buffs are removed when entering a raid.

### New race: Skyborne (es: "Cielonata")
- Elves from Zephras Isle, of Highborne origin. Players choose a faction at character creation: the Alliance branch "High Order Skyborne" (es: "de la Suma Orden", arcane) or the Horde branch "Windshaper Skyborne" (es: "Formavientos", elemental).
- Classes: warrior, hunter, rogue, druid (both factions); shaman Horde only; mage Alliance only. Their own druid forms.
- They start on Zephras Isle (1-12).
- Racials, both branches: Walk on Air (es: "Caminar en el aire"; active, glide downward for 10 sec, 1.5 min cooldown), Wind Blessed (passive, +2% haste) and Elemental Insight (passive, +5% damage against Elementals).
- High Order only: Read Leyline (es: "Leer línea ley"; +50% health and mana regeneration for 15 sec, 2 min cooldown; near a leyline you become Attuned for 1 hour and it gives +100%). Windshaper only: Skysight (+15% movement and mounted speed, 2 min cooldown; near an elemental convergence you become Charged for 1 hour and it gives +30%).

### Other changes
- New race and class combinations: gnome priest, human hunter, dwarf shaman, orc mage, troll warlock, undead paladin.
- Talents from level 10, with milestones at 11, 16, 21 and 31. Some classic buffs (Divine Spirit, Blessing of Kings, Improved Mark of the Wild) are part of the base class.
- Each race has two active and two passive racials.
- Burning Crusade-style riding: the cost is in the training, and each step gives a free mount (Wowhead, Blizzard interview):
  - Level 40, Apprentice Riding: 100 gold, includes your race's basic mount (+60% speed); extra colors 20 gold each.
  - Level 60, Journeyman Riding: 1,000 gold, includes your race's swift mount (+100% speed); extra swift mounts 200 gold each. About 1,000 gold in all, not 1,200.
  - Trained from your own race's riding trainer, next to its mount vendor (e.g. human: Eastvale Logging Camp, Elwynn Forest; dwarf: Amberstill Ranch, Dun Morogh; gnome: Steelgrill's Depot, Dun Morogh; night elf: Cenarion Enclave, Darnassus; Skyborne: Fairweather Stables, Zephras Isle; orc: Valley of Honor, Orgrimmar; troll: Sen'jin Village, Durotar; tauren: Bloodhoof Village, Mulgore; undead: Brill, Tirisfal Glades). Other races' mounts need Exalted reputation.
  - Honored with your home faction: 10% off the training and your race's mounts.
  - Riding seems to be learned per mount type: the in-game quest text says each creature needs its own riding skill, and per guides another race's mounts need Exalted with that race plus its own training. Some guides describe one riding skill for every mount: not settled.
  - Paladins and warlocks get their first mount free from a level 40 class quest, and skip the 100 gold training: paladin "The Tome of Nobility" (Duthorian Rall, Stormwind) or, undead, "A Paladin's Steed" (Danitha Morr, Tirisfal Glades); warlock "Summon Felsteed" (Strahad Farsan, above Ratchet), which grants Apprentice Riding (Blizzard hotfix). Since November 25 their level 60 epic mount quests also grant Journeyman Riding (Blizzard).
- Camps: the cooking fire becomes a shared campfire with camp bonuses. "Well Fed" gives +5% experience from kills.
- Legacy: account points (from classes, professions, PvP, exploration, dungeons and raids) spent on per-character perks.
- Optional transmog, HD/SD graphics, controller support. Realm rules: Normal, PvP and RP; Hardcore mode is announced for later, still with no date as of November 27, 2026.
- PvP: new battleground Darkspear Islands (15 vs 15) besides Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin and Alterac Valley; revamped honor system.
- Dungeon experience: a November 8, 2026 hotfix raised XP from dungeon kills by 50%. Since November 14, no one in the party gets XP from an enemy when a party member is more than 12 levels above it (no boosting).
- PvP: the blue honor gear needs no rank (per guides, level 55 to wear it; cloaks unlock at rank 3); epic pieces need an Emboldened Seal with a rank requirement.
- Hotfixes up to November 25, 2026 (patch 1.60.1; build 71139 live on November 27):
  - Balance Druid talent rework on November 25 (Moonfury replaced by Dreamstate, Starlight Wrath and Moon and Stars added, Improved Starfire 2 points; druids with 3 or more points in it got a talent reset).
  - World buffs no longer fall off when changing zones (November 23). Upper Blackrock Spire is back to its intended difficulty and Alterac Valley quests need level 58 (November 17).
  - Rogue poisons and Blinding Powder are temporarily Bind on Pickup; Alterac Valley gives much less for killing the General and more for objectives (November 25).
  - Vampiric Embrace heals for 10% of Shadow damage (was 20%); Windfury Totem attacks no longer ignore armor (November 16). Mortal Strike needs the talent (November 23).
